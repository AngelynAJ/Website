About Us

Nestled at 8 Market Place in Bungay, The Old Bank is a beloved tea room and cafe offering a unique experience with delicious homemade food, welcoming staff, and beautiful decor. We pride ourselves on generous portions, fair prices, and a warm atmosphere perfect for friends, families, and even your furry companions!
